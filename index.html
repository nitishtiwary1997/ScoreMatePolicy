ScoreMate Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

ScoreMate ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the ScoreMate mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To deliver a premium scoring experience, we may collect the following information:

  • Google account information (if you sign in with Google)
  • Match scores, stats, and cricket database entries
  • Device specifications, OS version, and network carrier
  • Crash logs and performance analytics data

2. How We Use Information

We utilize the collected details for the following purposes:

  • Provide live cricket scoring and record-keeping features
  • Safely store and synchronize matches across your devices
  • Optimize app performance, UI design, and features
  • Resolve crashes, bugs, and other technical limitations
  • Analyze global trends to plan future feature implementations

3. Google Sign-In

If you select to authenticate using Google Sign-In, we will request access to basic profile fields:

  • Your display name
  • Your email address
  • Your Google profile photo URL

Note: This information is strictly used to authenticate your login sessions and ensure your saved matches are backed up securely.

4. Advertisements

ScoreMate may show ads provided by Google AdMob. These mobile ad providers may collect device advertising IDs (like Google AAID) and basic system diagnostics to present personalized or non-personalized advertisements according to their own developer agreements and policies.

5. Third-Party Services

We leverage various trusted third-party SDKs and systems within our mobile app:

  • Google AdMob (Advertisement display)
  • Firebase Analytics (App metrics and crash insights)
  • Firebase Authentication (Secure user login session management)
  • Google Play Services (Essential platform functionalities)
  • Data Security

6. Data Security

We implement strong industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards (like SSL encryption and secure Firestore Rules) to prevent loss, misuse, modification, or unauthorized exposure of your match data.

7. Children’s Privacy

ScoreMate does not intentionally solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If we learn that a user under 13 has shared profile details without parental approval, we take steps to wipe that information immediately from our database logs.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to modify or change this policy description at any time. When updates occur, they will be instantly published. We recommend reviewing this document periodically to keep updated on our security measures.
